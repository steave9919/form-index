 EDITORIAL STANDARD v0.1

Scoring and evidence rules

Keep sources separate

Brand claims, platform tests, and user feedback use different fields and never substitute for one another.

Limit every claim

Findings apply only to the named model and sample. They are not generalized to an entire brand.

Retain evidence

Orders, batch details, original photos, measurements, and edit history form the review evidence file.

Right to respond

Brands may add documents, correct facts, or publish a response. They cannot buy scores or remove legitimate criticism.

Tell us what is wrong or unsafe.

Use this form for factual corrections, privacy requests, account removal, safety concerns, harassment, impersonation, brand conduct or an appeal. The report is stored privately and is not published in the community.

Urgent personal danger: contact local emergency services instead of waiting for a website report.

Do not upload or paste intimate images, identity documents, passwords, order numbers, tracking numbers, addresses or payment details. A URL and a concise factual description are enough to begin.
If the issue is about a profile or reply, link to the nearest public page.
Minimum 20 characters. Do not include private information that is not necessary.

What happens next

We review the linked content and evidence.

  1. Credible privacy, consent or serious safety reports can lead to immediate hiding while review continues.
  2. Corrections are checked against the current source; material changes may update the page and its review date.
  3. Privacy and deletion requests may require account verification, but not a password or identity document in the first message.
  4. Appeals can introduce new evidence. A decision is not final merely because the first report was closed.

We aim to acknowledge standard reports within two business days.

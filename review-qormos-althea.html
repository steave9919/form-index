Full Review

Althea is the easiest product in this QORMOS set to plan around. At a published 12.7 lb, it is far lighter than the 32 lb Lea or 70.4 lb Pink Paradise. The roughly 14 × 8 inch footprint should make storage and drying easier for a buyer who does not want a large body taking over a room.

The product page is detailed about dimensions and channel lengths, which is useful. It is less consistent about material: one section calls it TPR, while branding elsewhere says Bio TPE. Those terms are not interchangeable in every buyer's mind, and the page does not provide a material certification. We would ask support to confirm the exact body material before ordering.

The homepage promises a 90-day quality warranty, while this product page says 30-day quality assurance. That may reflect different programs, but the buyer should treat the written product-page terms as the baseline until QORMOS confirms otherwise. The strengths are price and handling, not documentation consistency.

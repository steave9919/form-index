Full Review

The Whitney is the entry point of the trio: a 17.4 lb silicone companion with a curved silhouette and elongated legs that give you more grip and position options. The dual-position design works held or placed flat.

The trade-off is weight — it does not have the grounded, full-size presence of a 1:1 model, but that is exactly what makes it the easiest to move, clean and store. For the price, it is the most approachable silicone companion Quzio sells.

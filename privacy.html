What we do not ask for

  • Real names, identity documents or proof of a sexual orientation.
  • Home addresses, phone numbers, order numbers or payment-card details.
  • Intimate photos or explicit descriptions of sexual activity.
  • Precise location data or unrelated health records.

Do not place this information in a post, comment or report. If it has already been posted, request removal and avoid repeating the data elsewhere.

How accounts and community data work

Account authentication and community records are processed through Supabase. Published posts and comments are designed to be readable without an account and may be indexed by search or AI systems. Your email address and raw voting record are not part of the public post view.

Votes are associated with an account to prevent duplicate voting and are exposed only through restricted aggregate functions where implemented. Administrators can restrict or remove content for moderation and safety reasons.

How reports work

The report form can be used without publishing the issue in the community. Reports are inserted into a restricted table, not into public posts. A contact email is optional; if you leave it blank, we may be unable to follow up or ask for more information.

Do not include intimate images, identity documents, order screenshots or unnecessary personal history. Include only the URL, the relevant fact and the minimum information needed to investigate.

Storage and essential processing

The current site uses browser storage to remember the age-gate confirmation and, when you sign in, authentication state through the community service. We do not currently describe these as advertising cookies. Selecting a retailer link takes you to a separate website that may use its own cookies and tracking under its own notice.

Current providers

GitHub Pages serves the static website. Supabase provides account, database and restricted report storage. jsDelivr may deliver the browser library used to connect to the community service.

Current build

No third-party advertising analytics are intentionally installed in this build. Hosting, authentication and database providers may still process technical request data to deliver and secure the service.

Retention

Account and community data are kept while the account or community record is active, then retained only as reasonably needed for security, moderation, dispute handling or legal obligations. Reports are retained only while needed to investigate, document a decision or respond to an appeal.

Published content may remain in third-party search caches, archives or copies outside FORM INDEX after deletion on the site. We can remove our copy; we cannot guarantee immediate removal from independent systems.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, or request a portable copy of personal data. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

Use the report form and select Privacy or account request. Describe the account, content URL or specific right you want to exercise. We may need to verify control of the account before acting. Do not send a password or identity document in the initial request.

Security and age limit

We use restricted database policies, private report access and browser-based authentication. No service can promise absolute security. Report suspected account compromise or data exposure promptly.

The community is for adults aged 18 or older. An age-gate confirmation is not a guarantee that every participant is an adult, so reports involving suspected minors are treated as urgent safety matters.

Changes to this notice

When the data practices change, the effective date and the relevant page will be updated. A material change may also require a community notice before it takes effect.

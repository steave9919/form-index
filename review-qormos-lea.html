Full Review

Lea is the product we would shortlist for someone who finds a 12 lb torso too light but does not want to manage the 70.4 lb Pink Paradise. The published 31.96 lb / 14.5 kg weight gives it enough mass to stay supported during use while remaining in a different handling class from the largest model.

The 1:1-scale claim needs interpretation: Lea is a torso, not a complete body with a head and full limbs. Its value is concentrated in the torso proportions, width and rounded shape. QORMOS publishes the weight, but not a complete dimension set or a shipping-box size, so measure a storage and drying area before ordering.

The biggest problem is transactional rather than physical. QORMOS's homepage advertised Lea at $429.99, but the product page rendered $0.00 at the time of this check. We would not place the product record at a confirmed price until the live variant price is visible and consistent. That is enough to lower the confidence score even though the product concept is sensible.

Full Review

The Cora is the flagship of Quzio's companion line: a 1:1 anatomical build at 39.6 lb (18 kg) with a gel-filled chest, lifelike skin detailing, and three textured channels. The extra mass gives it a grounded, stable feel that the lighter models cannot match.

That presence has a cost in handling — it is the most demanding to lift, dry and store, so plan storage space before buying. For buyers who want realism and weight, it is the obvious top end of this range.

Full Review

Pink Paradise is not a casual upgrade from a compact torso. At 70.4 lb, moving it wet, turning it for drying and returning it to storage become the main ownership questions. The buyer needs a stable support surface, enough floor space and a plan for cleaning that does not require carrying the full weight across a room.

The published channel lengths and product weight are useful, but the listing does not state overall body dimensions or the shipping-box dimensions. That makes it difficult to check a doorway, storage cabinet or drying area in advance. We would not treat the 1:1 label as a complete space specification.

QORMOS also uses TPR in this product's specification list and Bio TPE in the generic specification block. Until the exact material is confirmed in writing, buyers should avoid assuming it supports the same cleaning or longevity expectations as a clearly documented silicone product. The visual concept is strong; the documentation and handling burden keep the score lower.

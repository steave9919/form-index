How to Choose a Companion Product

A straightforward guide for first-time and returning buyers.

1. Choose your material

TPE is the soft, flexible and more affordable option, but it is porous and needs thorough drying and light powdering after cleaning. Silicone is less porous, more tear-resistant and easier to keep clean, at the cost of a firmer feel and a higher price.

2. Full-size doll or torso

Full-size companion dolls give you a complete body, natural posing and more realism, but they weigh more and take longer to dry. Torsos and silicone companions trade some presence for much easier handling, cleaning and storage.

3. Check weight and storage

Weight is the spec most buyers underestimate. A 39.6 lb model needs a stable surface and a plan for moving and drying it. If you have limited space, a 17.4 lb model is far easier to live with.

4. Care essentials

  • Rinse and dry completely after every use.
  • Lightly powder TPE and store it away from dark fabrics and heat.
  • Keep the product in a breathable cover, not sealed plastic.

Buying Guide FAQ

What is the difference between TPE and silicone?

TPE is softer and usually cheaper but needs careful drying and powdering. Silicone is more tear-resistant, less porous and easier to keep clean, but often firmer and more expensive.

Should I buy a full-size doll or a torso?

Choose a full-size doll for realism and posing. Choose a torso if you want lower weight, easier cleaning and simpler storage.

How do I clean and store a companion product?

Rinse after each use with warm water and mild cleaner, dry completely, lightly powder, and store in a breathable cover away from heat and dark fabrics.

Editorial rules

  • Brand claims, platform tests and user feedback stay in separate fields.
  • Findings apply only to the named model, not the whole brand.
  • Orders, measurements, photos and edit history form the evidence file.
  • Brands may correct facts or respond, but cannot buy scores or remove criticism.

Methodology FAQ

How are FORM INDEX editor scores calculated?

Each product is scored on five weighted criteria — material (15%), build and durability (20%), cleaning and upkeep (20%), real-world experience (30%), and support and value (15%).

Are the reviews independent lab tests?

No. Scores are editorial estimates based on published retailer specifications and verified-buyer feedback. They are not independent laboratory tests.

Does FORM INDEX earn commissions?

Yes. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases through retailer links, and this does not change any score.

What FORM INDEX is

Research

A practical buying and ownership resource

We translate published specifications into the questions buyers actually face: Can one person move it? How long does drying take? What has to be stored? Which feature is useful and which is marketing?

Coverage starts with companion dolls, silicone companions and the care routines around them.

Community

A place for specific ownership experience

Members can ask focused questions and report what changed after days, weeks or months of ownership. We value material behavior, handling, maintenance, support and value — not promotional first impressions.

Community content stays factual and non-explicit so it remains useful, safer to moderate and easier to cite.

What it is not

Not an independent laboratory

Unless a page explicitly says otherwise, scores are editorial estimates. We do not claim laboratory testing, medical certification or universal material safety.

Not a retailer or an adult social network

We do not sell the products ourselves. We also do not host explicit content, private chat, dating profiles or non-consensual imagery.

How FORM INDEX is funded

We may earn a commission when a reader buys through a retailer link. Commercial relationships are disclosed near the relevant link. Affiliate revenue does not buy a score, a ranking, a positive review or the removal of criticism.

Retailers and brands can correct factual errors or provide a response. The correction is assessed against evidence and disclosed when it changes the page.

When we add retailers and products

Coverage expands only after a catalog and policy review. Before adding a retailer, we check whether the product identity and selected bundle are clear, whether material and care claims can be sourced, and whether shipping, return, warranty and privacy policies can be reviewed.

Current coverage starts with Quzio's companion range. That is a statement about the current catalog, not an endorsement of every product or policy on the retailer's site.

Corrections are welcome. If a price, specification, retailer policy or ownership claim is wrong, use the report form. Include the page URL and the source that supports the correction.

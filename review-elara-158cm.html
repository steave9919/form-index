Full Review

The Elara is Quzio's near-premium full-size option, distinguished by its red-haired, blue-eyed sculpt and a posing range that reads closer to a premium companion build. It shares the same articulated skeleton and three-channel design as the Aline but leans into facial realism.

At $553.60 on sale it still sits under $600, with the same plain-packaging and 1-year-warranty basics. The choice between Aline and Elara is mostly aesthetic and articulation: pick Elara if the face and posing matter more than saving a few dollars.

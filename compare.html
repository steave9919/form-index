Comparison FAQ

Which Quzio companion doll is the best value?

The Aline 157cm. It offers a full-size TPE body, articulated skeleton and three-channel design for under $600.

What is the difference between a companion doll and a silicone companion?

Full-size companion dolls have a complete body and articulated skeleton. Silicone companions are partial torsos that prioritise weight, handling and texture.

Which model is easiest to clean?

The Whitney Torso. At 17.4 lb it is the lightest and quickest to rinse, dry and store.

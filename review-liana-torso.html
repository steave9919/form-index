Full Review

The Liana is a 1:1 life-size silicone companion with a tear-resistant, corrosion-proof surface and a textured channel design. The standout is value: it ships with a full accessory care kit (rinser, UV wand, absorbent sticks and more), so the essentials are covered from day one.

At about 28.7 lb it has realistic weight but needs a stable surface for use and drying, and it takes a little more effort to move than compact models. If you want a full-size silicone companion and care about texture, it is the strongest all-round pick of the three.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included with the Liana?

The torso plus a free accessory care kit: automatic rinser, UV sterilization wand, absorbent sticks, suction tube, charging cable and manual.

How much does the Liana weigh?

About 28.7 lb (13 kg).

Is it easy to clean?

Yes. The silicone surface is non-porous and quick to rinse, though the size means drying takes a little longer.

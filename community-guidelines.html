Useful experience, shared without giving up privacy.

The community exists to make adult-product purchases and ownership easier to understand. Keep discussion factual, non-explicit and respectful. Do not reveal private information about yourself or anyone else.

Effective: November 25, 2026 · Applies to posts, comments, profiles, votes, reports and brand participation

The short version: adults only; no explicit content; no minors or non-consensual imagery; no doxxing; no fake owners; no undisclosed brand promotion; no medical diagnosis. Report urgent safety or privacy issues immediately.

What belongs here

  • Specific buying questions about size, weight, materials, storage, delivery or value.
  • First-hand ownership updates that explain what changed over time.
  • Cleaning, drying, maintenance and repair experiences described in practical terms.
  • Retailer support, packaging, warranty and return outcomes without personal identifiers.
  • Corrections supported by a source, product page or reproducible observation.
  • Clear disagreement about a product or review that focuses on evidence rather than the person.

Content standards

Write for someone who needs to make a practical decision. A useful post identifies the model and material, explains how long you have owned it and separates observation from assumption.

Prefer this

“After six weeks, the TPE surface became tacky faster than expected. I dry it for 25 minutes and powder it lightly after every wash.”

Do not post this

Explicit sexual detail, graphic photos, descriptions of minors, erotic role-play, unsolicited fantasies or any content involving a real person without consent.

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Anyone under 18, content that sexualizes minors, or attempts to determine or expose a member's age through private information.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual deepfakes, impersonation, threats, stalking, harassment or encouragement of self-harm.
  • Doxxing or private data, including names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, order numbers, tracking numbers, payment details, workplace details or identifiable photos.
  • Explicit content, graphic sexual descriptions, fetish solicitation, dating requests, private messaging and attempts to move conversations off-platform for sexual contact.
  • Fake ownership or reviews, purchased votes, coordinated manipulation, copied reviews, undisclosed incentives or accounts operated by a seller without disclosure.
  • Unsupported safety claims, including medical cures, guaranteed disease prevention, “100% safe” claims or instructions that conflict with the manufacturer's care guidance.
  • Spam and exploitation, including bulk promotion, malicious links, scraping, credential collection, counterfeit sales and attempts to bypass moderation.
Do not post private purchase evidence. A screenshot of an order, shipping label or support email can expose your identity, address or payment details. Describe what happened; do not upload the original document.

Privacy and personal safety

Use a pseudonym

Your public username and posts can be seen by anyone. Use a name that is not reused with your real identity and do not include personal details in a post.

Share only what is necessary

For a correction or safety report, include the page URL and the relevant fact. Do not send intimate images, identity documents or unrelated personal history.

If you believe you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This community is not an emergency responder.

Brands, retailers and affiliates

Commercial participants must identify themselves. An official account may answer factual questions, provide a correction, describe a policy or explain a support outcome. It may not pose as an owner, post undisclosed recommendations, offer an incentive for a positive review or pressure a member to remove criticism.

Commercial relationships between FORM INDEX and a retailer must be disclosed near the relevant link. A brand response does not become an editor score, and a brand cannot buy placement or deletion.

Moderation and enforcement

  1. Review

    Reports are checked against the content, the linked source and the surrounding thread.

  2. Immediate protection when needed

    Content may be hidden while a credible privacy, consent or serious safety concern is investigated.

  3. Action

    Depending on the issue, we may correct the content, hide it, remove it, warn the account, restrict posting or suspend access.

  4. Appeal

    A member or brand may request review of the decision and supply new evidence. Repeated abuse of the reporting process is itself a moderation issue.

We aim to acknowledge standard reports within two business days. Complex privacy or safety reviews may take longer. Immediate removal is not a finding that every allegation is proven.

Content ownership and removal

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you give FORM INDEX permission to display, format, index and archive the content as part of the community. You can request removal of your own content or personal information through the report page. Removal may not erase third-party caches or copies outside FORM INDEX.
